## New arithmetic

### Acquisition yield

For alternate round r, marginal yield is `(N[r] - N[r-1]) / ((bytes[r] - bytes[r-1]) / 1e9)`.

| Round | Added opportunities | Additional bytes | Opportunities per decimal GB |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 11 | 610,514,454 | 18.02 |
| 2 | 9 | 596,205,693 | 15.10 |
| 3 | 11 | 590,329,239 | 18.63 |
| 4 | 5 | 617,772,188 | 8.09 |

These ratios summarize the executed acquisition sequence. They do not compare unexecuted strategies, establish an optimal policy, or constitute an additional acquisition experiment.

### Cell and total shortfalls

For Figure 6, each source cell contributes `max(8 - N[s,b], 0)`. Summing within stations yields 39, 18, 11, 26, 40, 10, 21 and 6, totaling 171. Retaining the original 150 opportunities and filling every deficient cell exactly gives 321; one existing nine-opportunity cell explains why this is not 320. Another 79 would be required to reach 400. The derivation preserves all 40 source cells in `data/capacity_cell_shortfalls.csv`; `data/capacity_shortfall_summary.json` records source hashes and interpretation. It does not show that the required opportunities exist or can be acquired within the original budget.
