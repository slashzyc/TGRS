\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The audit question was defined after the source capacity failure was known. Although v5 inherited the file universe, thresholds, and outcome restriction without alteration, it remains an exploratory analysis. The earlier local freeze was not public preregistration, and the study did not undergo the prospective journal review associated with a Registered Report. Subsequent verification cannot change that chronology.

The sample covers one 140-day period, eight evaluation stations, four development stations, and 128 files selected by one policy. Its conclusions depend on the 12.5-km tolerance, candidate rules, complete alternate rounds, and 400/8 thresholds. The counts are complete for that design, but they do not estimate the frequency of readiness failures across the NOAA archive, other coastal sectors, or other sampling policies. The thresholds also lack a formal power or precision derivation.

Several design and data-access mechanisms remain inseparable. Within the selected study interval, 141 of 560 date-prefix requests had the joint label \texttt{public\_\allowbreak endpoint\_\allowbreak empty\_\allowbreak or\_\allowbreak not\_\allowbreak backfilled}. These 560 requests are a subset of the broader 1,232-request listing inventory, not a replacement denominator. The joint label does not distinguish absent observations from listing or backfill state. Likewise, terminal counts combine station location, file selection, spatial tolerance, candidate eligibility, and processing rules. They cannot establish why an observation was unavailable or whether the same location would be supported under another design.

The outcome restriction limits the scientific quantities evaluated. Repository counters report zero NDBC wind-outcome reads, and the replay found no outcome path or field. Wind error, ambiguity correctness, candidate containment, calibration, energy score, selection regret, and baseline ranking were therefore not measured. The intended performance claims remain untested. The record also cannot prove that an external process or person never accessed outcomes.

Provenance and reproducibility have separate gaps. As described in Section~\ref{sec:provenance}, one receipt retains a deleted staging locator, and successful transfers lack individual probe receipts. The raw-file deletions additionally prevent spatial sensitivity analyses from the retained extracts alone. Digest agreement and aggregate byte reconciliation do not resolve those omissions. The transaction checks provide logical closure within the cooperating workflow, not protection against a noncooperative concurrent writer or power-loss atomicity.

Finally, source verification and the claim-level audit shared the original repository and implementation context. The reported non-owner verification does not amount to an independent reproduction. The original research pack is absent from the current public companion release, and its code, test results, and artifact hashes have not been independently rechecked here. The literature assessment was also a targeted evidence map of 15 sources with two later interoperability additions, rather than a systematic review. These limits preclude stronger claims about methodological priority, external reproducibility, or operational readiness.
