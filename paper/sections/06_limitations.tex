\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The audit was defined after the source capacity failure was known and remains exploratory. The earlier repository-local freeze was neither public preregistration nor prospective journal review as a Registered Report.

The sample covers one 140-day period, eight evaluation and four development stations, and 128 files selected by one policy. Results depend on the 12.5-km tolerance, candidate rules, complete alternate rounds, and configured 400/8 thresholds. They do not estimate readiness failures across the NOAA archive or validate those thresholds as sufficient for a scientific comparison.

The joint empty-or-not-backfilled label covered 141 of 560 study-interval date-prefix requests within the 1,232-request inventory, without distinguishing observation absence from listing or backfill state. Terminal counts combine location, file selection, tolerance, candidate eligibility, and processing rules. The 211 evaluation records with invalid time require underlying examples and time-decoding checks before their causes can be identified.

The source regional labels do not establish product coverage. Station 42056's Gulf selection assignment requires verification against actual file footprints; Supplementary Table~S1 retains the original label and identifies this unresolved mapping. The candidate station universe, region eligibility boundaries, hash configuration, file manifest, and extraction-field definitions are also needed to reconstruct selection and processing.

Repository counters report zero NDBC wind-outcome reads, and the replay found no outcome path or field. Wind error, ambiguity correctness, calibration, and model rankings were not measured. The record cannot establish whether any external process or person accessed outcomes.

Section~\ref{sec:provenance} describes a deleted staging locator and absent individual probe receipts. Raw-file deletions also prevent spatial sensitivity analysis from the retained extracts alone. Digest agreement and aggregate byte reconciliation do not resolve these omissions. The transaction checks provide closure within the cooperating workflow, not power-loss atomicity or protection against a noncooperative writer.

Source verification and the claim-level audit shared the original repository and implementation, so the non-owner review does not establish independent reproduction. The original research pack is absent from the public companion release; its code, tests, and hashes have not been independently rechecked here. The targeted literature map was not a systematic review and cannot establish methodological priority.
