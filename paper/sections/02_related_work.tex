\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Coastal ASCAT observations}
ASCAT is a C-band scatterometer carried by the MetOp satellites. Its two observing swaths are separated by a central gap, so nominal global coverage cannot be read as a guarantee of observations at a particular coastal station \cite{eumetsat2022,wmo2026}. Coastal wind retrieval also involves resolution, land-contamination, and quality-control choices. Verhoef \emph{et al.}\ studied high-resolution ASCAT winds near the coast \cite{verhoef2012}, and Lindsley \emph{et al.}\ analyzed and validated high-resolution retrievals \cite{lindsley2016}. More recently, Soisuvarn \emph{et al.}\ described the NOAA coastal processor, including land correction, quality control, and validation \cite{soisuvarn2023}.

These studies provide the product context for the present analysis. They are not performance baselines for this audit: the audit recorded no reference-wind access and did not score a wind model. Its question is whether the chosen files, stations, time blocks, and extraction rules yield the number of predictor opportunities required by a particular evaluation plan. Product validation and benchmark support are complementary forms of evidence.

\subsection{Analysis-ready data and fitness for purpose}
Analysis-ready data organize observations so that users can perform analyses without repeating substantial preprocessing \cite{dwyer2018}. Quality-assurance guidance emphasizes traceability and the evidence needed to judge fitness for purpose \cite{qa4eo2010}. Dataset documentation extends this concern to composition, provenance, intended uses, and limitations \cite{gebru2021}. Remote-sensing reproducibility studies similarly emphasize transparent methods and accessible materials \cite{kedron2022}.

Readiness for a benchmark adds a design-specific requirement to these ideas. Even consistently processed data may leave a station or time block without enough eligible observations. The rule studied here specifies how support is counted and what must happen when it is insufficient. The distinction avoids treating the existence of downloadable files as evidence that a planned comparison is adequately supported.

\subsection{Prespecification and workflow provenance}
Prespecification helps distinguish an analysis planned before outcomes are examined from an analysis motivated by an observed result \cite{nosek2018}. Registered Reports additionally involve journal review of the study plan before results are known \cite{chambers2015}. The source protocol was locally prespecified before recorded wind-outcome access, but the study was neither publicly preregistered nor conducted as a Registered Report. This audit was defined after the capacity result and is exploratory.

Provenance frameworks offer ways to describe the relationship between plans, data, software, and execution. PROV-DM defines a general model of entities, activities, agents, and derivations \cite{prov2013}. RO-Crate packages research objects and their metadata \cite{rocrate2022}, while Workflow Run RO-Crate records the provenance of workflow executions \cite{leo2024}. BagIt addresses file packaging and checksum-based fixity \cite{bagit2018}. The implementation examined here uses hashes, typed records, and run receipts for related purposes, but does not claim conformance to those standards.

Discovery and computation operate at another layer. The SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog (STAC) standard describes spatiotemporal assets and their links \cite{stac2025}; cloud-native repositories support scalable access to scientific arrays \cite{abernathey2021}. These approaches can carry inputs and provenance for a readiness assessment. The application still has to define its own counting unit, support thresholds, repair policy, and decision semantics.

The original study's literature map was a targeted review of 15 sources assembled on 4 November 2026. Two interoperability references were added on 12 November 2026. The original bounded search cannot establish exhaustive novelty. We therefore describe the work as an integration of existing principles, with an explicit implementation and a documented coastal ASCAT case.
